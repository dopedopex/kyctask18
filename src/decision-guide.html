Redbelly KYC decision guide

Do I need KYC?

KYC means Redbelly Access, with the identity check by Averer. It is needed for anything you do on Redbelly mainnet.

Not needed

  • Buy, hold or trade RBNT on an exchangeFor example Gate or MEXC
  • Hold, send or swap RBNT on another chainBridged RBNT or wRBNT on Ethereum, Base, BNB Chain and others

Needed

  • Bridge RBNT onto Redbelly mainnet
  • Bridge RBNT off Redbelly mainnet
  • Send, swap or use apps on Redbelly mainnet
  • Stake RBNT on Redbelly mainnet
  • Vote in the Redbelly DAO

One line test: if you never send a transaction on Redbelly mainnet (chain ID 151), you do not need Redbelly KYC.

Approval

Usually a few minutes, in rare cases a few days

Wallets

Up to 10 per verified identity

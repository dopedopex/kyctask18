Redbelly KYC in 30 seconds

NEEDED (verify and enable your wallet at access.redbelly.network): send, swap or use apps, bridge onto or off Redbelly mainnet, or stake on Redbelly mainnet.
NOT NEEDED: holding RBNT on an exchange, or wrapped or bridged RBNT on another chain.

Approval time: usually a few minutes, in rare cases a few days (Redbelly Access’s own wording while pending). Press “Check verification status”. Still pending after a few days: open an Averer ticket at https://averer-help.freshdesk.com/support/tickets/new

Wallets: up to 10 per verified identity. At 10 the portal shows “Account allocation exhausted.”

Regions: the DAO is open to every region (Snapshot vote closed 5 July 2026). Redbelly Network still does not offer services in the clause 15.1 jurisdictions. These are two separate rules.

Never share your seed phrase, private key or ID photos.
Full guide: https://pykyc.vercel.app/

REDBELLY KYC: DO YOU NEED IT?

NOT NEEDED: RBNT on an exchange. RBNT or wRBNT on another chain (Ethereum, Base, Solana and others).
NEEDED: sending, swapping or using apps on Redbelly mainnet, bridging off mainnet, staking, DAO voting.
BRIDGING ONTO MAINNET: verify and enable your wallet first. Whether an unverified wallet can receive bridged coins is unconfirmed.

Wallet limit: 10 wallets per verified identity.
Approval: usually a few minutes, rare cases a few days.

Regions (two separate rules):
- DAO: general membership is open from any region.
- Redbelly Network: not offered in the 18 jurisdictions in clause 15.1 of its terms.

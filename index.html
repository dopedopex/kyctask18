Redbelly KYC and wallet activation, in plain language

Do you need KYC, how long does it take, how many wallets can you add, and what do you do when it gets stuck. Every answer links to the page it came from.

Two different regional rules exist. Keep them apart.

The DAO

Open to every region for general DAO members. The DAO vote to remove geographic restrictions closed on 5 July 2026.[S10]

Redbelly Network

Still does not offer services in the jurisdictions listed in clause 15.1 of its terms. The DAO vote did not change this.[S6] See question 4.

Labels: OFFICIAL comes from an official Redbelly or Averer page. PORTAL is wording shown by Redbelly Access itself. COMMUNITY and unconfirmed mean no official page confirms it. Last updated 22 October 2026. Screens can change, so if a live screen differs from this page, follow the live screen.

Part A. Do I need KYC?

“KYC” here means Redbelly Access, with identity checks by Averer. It lets a wallet send transactions on Redbelly Network. Find what you want to do.

Needed verify and enable your wallet first. Not needed no Redbelly KYC involved. Staking through an exchange follows that exchange’s rules.

Native or wrapped: the one-line test

Native RBNT lives on Redbelly mainnet. Wrapped or bridged RBNT is a copy on another chain. If you never send a transaction on Redbelly mainnet, you do not need Redbelly KYC.[S7]

Part B. The five common questions

1. When do I need KYC, and when don’t I?

You need it to send transactions on Redbelly mainnet. You do not need it to hold RBNT on an exchange or wrapped RBNT on another chain.

Before a wallet can write to the network, its owner claims an access credential from an accredited issuer and then enables the wallet.[S1] [S3] Averer is the issuer that checks your identity on Redbelly Access.[S5] The Part A table covers each situation.

2. How many wallets can I add to one KYC?

Up to 10 wallets per verified identity. At 10, the portal stops you adding more.

After your first wallet is enabled, Redbelly Access offers Add another account and Link another wallet, so more wallets join the same verified identity with no new check.[S5] [E4] When 10 accounts are linked, the portal shows “Account allocation exhausted.” and the add option goes away.[E1] The limit is set by the Redbelly Access portal itself, so a wallet past 10 cannot be added.

You are accountable for what every linked wallet does on the network, so only link wallets you control.[E4] If you need more than 10, no official page describes a way. Ask through Part D.

3. How long does KYC approval take?

Usually a few minutes. In rare cases, a few days.

This is the portal’s own wording while your check is pending: “This could take a few minutes but in some (rare) cases it could take a few days.”[E2] It is not a guaranteed time, and Averer’s help centre does not publish one. What to do depends on the screen you see:

  • “Identity verification pending”. Press Check verification status to refresh. If it is still pending after a few days, open a ticket with Averer.
  • “Identity verification incomplete”. Averer needs more from you. Press Retry identity verification and resubmit.[E2]
  • “Identity verification failed”. Open a ticket with Averer.[E2]
  • After approval, “It seems you haven’t received the RBNT yet”. This is a different, shorter wait for the RBNT that pays for enabling. Wait a few minutes and press RETRY.[E3]

4. Can I join from my country?

The DAO: yes, from any region. The network: not if you are in a clause 15.1 jurisdiction.

Redbelly DAO

The DAO vote “Increase Governance Accessibility and Protect Contributor Privacy” closed on 5 July 2026 and removed the regional restriction for general DAO members.[S10] Anyone can join the DAO from any region.

Redbelly Network

Clause 15.1 of the terms lists places where Redbelly does not offer services. Clause 15.2 says tax residents of, or people located in, those places may not use the website, platform and tools.[S6] Redbelly Access is part of that website.

The DAO vote changed the DAO’s own membership rule. It did not change the network’s terms.

5. Do I need KYC to stake?

Yes, to stake on Redbelly mainnet itself. Exchange or app staking follows that platform’s own rules.

Redbelly’s staking page describes node operators depositing 100,000 RBNT into a staking contract on the network, and every write to Redbelly needs an enabled wallet.[S8] [S1] Staking on an exchange is not a Redbelly transaction. For a third-party staking app no official page says, so treat it as unconfirmed and ask the app.

Part C. Get verified and enable a wallet

You do this once at access.redbelly.network. Redbelly Access is the official portal. Averer is the accredited issuer that checks your identity. The three stages are wallet connection, the KYC check and on-chain permission.[S4] Button names below are written as the portal shows them.[E3]

Before you start
  • MetaMask or Coinbase Wallet. These are the wallets the portal names.[S5]
  • A valid passport. Verification uses a photo ID with a biometric check.[S1]
  • The address you want to use on Redbelly mainnet. The account you connect becomes your public address there.
  • No fee for you in the usual case. The issuer covers the fees to grant access, and sends the RBNT that pays for enabling.[S5] [E3]
  • Check the clause 15.1 jurisdictions first. The terms do not allow people there to use the platform.
  1. Open the portal and connect your walletGo to access.redbelly.network, press Connect wallet and approve the connection in your wallet.[E3]
  2. If the wallet will not connect, add the network by handPress ADD NETWORK MANUALLY or enter these details in your wallet: network name Redbelly Network Mainnet, RPC URL https://governors.mainnet.redbelly.network, chain ID 151, symbol RBNT.[S9] [E3]
  3. Start verificationOn the “Get access credential” screen press Begin identity verification.[E3]
  4. Read the notice and continue to AvererThe portal says Averer manages the check. Tick I understand, then press Continue with Averer.[E3]
  5. Sign the request in your walletYour wallet asks you to let Averer see your public address. Sign it.[E3]
  6. Complete the check with AvererA window titled “Interacting with averer.co” opens. Follow it to submit your passport and finish the biometric check. Enter your details only here.[S1] [E3]
  7. Wait for approvalThe screen shows “Identity verification pending”. This usually takes a few minutes, and rarely a few days. Press Check verification status to refresh.[E2]
  8. Enable your accountAfter approval the account shows “Account status: Not enabled”. Press Enable account and sign the transaction in your wallet. If the portal says “It seems you haven’t received the RBNT yet”, wait a few minutes and press RETRY.[E3]
  9. Check it workedThe account shows “Account status: Enabled” and the portal shows “Next steps”. This wallet can now send transactions, bridge and stake on Redbelly mainnet.[E3]

Open Redbelly Access

Add another wallet to your verified identity

You do not verify again. Open the portal with a wallet that is already enabled and use the Accounts section. You can link up to 10 wallets in total.[E1]

Same wallet app, other address

Press Add another account. In your wallet, switch this site’s connection to the address you want to add, then sign the request.[E4]

A different wallet app

Press Link another wallet. Use the wallet button in the top right of the portal to connect the other wallet, then sign the request.[E4]

Then press Enable account for the new wallet, as in step 8. If the portal says the account “must first be funded with at least” a stated amount of RBNT, send that amount from one of your enabled wallets and try again.[E3]

Enabling works per network. After your first account is enabled, Redbelly Access also offers a Testnet account, so enable on the network you use.[S5] [S11]

This guide has no portal screenshots because the flow shows a real ID check and personal data.

Part D. When something goes wrong

The portal splits help in two. Problems with your identity check go to Averer. Problems connecting or enabling go to Redbelly Support.[E5]

Approval is taking too long

  1. Check you are on access.redbelly.network with the wallet you used to verify.
  2. Press Check verification status.
  3. The portal says most checks take a few minutes and rare ones a few days.[E2] If it still shows “Identity verification pending” after a few days, open a ticket with Averer.

Verification was incomplete or failed

Screen saysWhat it meansWhat to do
Identity verification incompleteAverer is “not yet able to verify your identity” and you may need to resubmit some information.Press Retry identity verification and resubmit. If it keeps happening, open a ticket.
Identity verification failedAverer “was not able to verify your identity”.Open a ticket with Averer. The portal’s CONTACT AVERER link goes to averer.co.

Wording as shown by the portal.[E2] No official page lists the reasons a check can fail, so do not assume one.

I reached the wallet limit

At 10 linked accounts the portal shows “Account allocation exhausted.” and hides Add another account.[E1] No official page describes a way past 10, so use one of the 10 you already have. If the portal says “The address you’re trying to add is attached to another account.”, that address is already linked to a different identity and cannot be added to yours.[E4]

The wallet will not connect or the account will not enable

Add the network by hand as in Part C step 2. If you see “It seems you haven’t received the RBNT yet”, wait a few minutes and press RETRY.[E3] If it still fails, use Redbelly Support.[S12]

How to open a support ticket with Averer

  1. Open the ticket formGo to the Averer ticket form. The help portal behind Contact support on averer.co is at averer-help.freshdesk.com.[S13] [S14]
  2. Fill in the formIt asks for your name, email address, a subject, a description and your Redbelly wallet address, and lets you attach files. Fields can change, so follow the form on screen.[S13]
  3. Say what the screen showsWrite what you tried and the exact message, for example “Identity verification failed”, and the public wallet address you used in Redbelly Access. A wrong address makes the ticket harder to match to your check.
  4. SubmitPress Submit after checking the details.
Never send your seed phrase, private key, or photos of your ID in a ticket, a message or a form. The form only needs your public wallet address.

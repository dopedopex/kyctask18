A plain-language guide to Redbelly Access KYC (verification by Averer) and wallet activation.

Live guide: https://pykyc.vercel.app/

## Files

- `index.html` - the full guide (Parts A to D, pin version, sources)
- `evidence.html` - the portal wording the guide relies on (E1 to E5)
- `decision-guide.png` / `decision-guide.svg` - Part A decision guide
- `pinned-short-version.txt` - short version for Discord or Telegram (matches Questions 2 and 3)

## Notes

- Two separate rules: the DAO is open to every region (Snapshot vote closed 5 July 2026), while Redbelly Network still excludes the clause 15.1 jurisdictions.
- Approval time and the 10-wallet limit come from Redbelly Access wording (E2, E1).
- Items marked “unconfirmed” have no official source.
- Always check the live official pages before acting.

Portal wording used in the guide

Redbelly Access is an app, so its screens are not normal web pages you can link to. This page lists the on-screen wording the guide relies on, so you can compare it with the live portal at access.redbelly.network. Screens can change. If the live screen differs, follow the live screen.

E1. Accounts screen: wallet limit

With 10 linked accounts the portal shows:

Account allocation exhausted.

and the Add another account option is no longer offered.

E2. Verification status messages

Pending (screen title “Identity verification pending”):

This could take a few minutes but in some (rare) cases it could take a few days.

Incomplete (screen title “Identity verification incomplete”): Averer is “not yet able to verify your identity. You may need to resubmit some information.” The button is Retry identity verification.

Failed (screen title “Identity verification failed”): Averer “was not able to verify your identity.” The portal links to CONTACT AVERER.

The refresh button on the pending screen is Check verification status.

E3. Screens and buttons, in order

  1. Connect wallet, and ADD NETWORK MANUALLY if the wallet does not connect.
  2. Screen “Get access credential”: Begin identity verification.
  3. Notice about Averer: tick I understand, then Continue with Averer.
  4. Wallet request to let Averer access your public address. Sign it.
  5. Window “Interacting with averer.co”.
  6. “Identity verification pending”, then Check verification status.
  7. “Account status: Not enabled”, then Enable account and sign the transaction.
  8. If shown: “It seems you haven’t received the RBNT yet”, then RETRY.
  9. “Account status: Enabled” and “Next steps”.
  10. For an extra wallet, if shown: the account “must first be funded with at least” a stated amount of RBNT.

E4. Adding wallets

Add another account links another address from the same wallet app. Link another wallet links a different wallet app, connected with the wallet button at the top right of the portal. The portal also warns that you are accountable for everything a linked wallet does on the network, and shows this error for an address already linked elsewhere:

The address you’re trying to add is attached to another account.

E5. “Having trouble?” help split

Problems with identity verification are directed to Averer. Problems connecting to the network or enabling an account are directed to Redbelly Support (vine.redbelly.network/support/).
